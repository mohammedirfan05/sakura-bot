Here are some ideas for a Discord ticket winner system that keeps everything organized and fair.

🏆 Custom Games Winner Ticket

When a player wins, they open (or staff creates) a 🏆 Winner Claim ticket.

Winner Information

* Epic Games Name:
* Discord Username:
* Game Mode Won:
    * Solo Reload Builds
    * Solo Reload Zero Build
    * Duo Reload
    * Other
* Date Won:
* Screenshot of Victory Royale (if required):

Staff Verification

* ✅ Winner confirmed
* ✅ Rules checked (no teaming, cheating, or exploiting)
* ✅ Win limit checked
* ✅ Prize approved

Prize Delivery

* V-Bucks Sent: ✅
* Sent By:
* Date Sent:
* Ticket Closed By:

⸻
